UEHGC - ZEEN AI SCAN

"UEHGC - Zeen AI Scan" is an application (App) that identifies, classifies, and provides educational information about waste using Artificial Intelligence (AI). This tool is designed to educate on consumer responsibility and facilitate quick and efficient waste segregation at the source.

AI TRASH BIN

AI Trash Bin is a flagship initiative, designed to catalyze a circular economy at Campus V. This patented device utilizes AI-driven visual recognition to accurately classify seven waste categories, while integrating weight sensors to feed real-time data into the Digital Twin for emissions tracking.

PLATFORM FOR GREEN FAIR

The Platform for Green Fair is a space for students to exchange used items, promoting reuse habits to reduce waste generation. This is a practical circular economy initiative that enables the student community to actively participate and co-create solutions to achieve the Net Zero Campus 2026 goal.
